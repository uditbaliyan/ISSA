Revisiting the disastrous debris flows of 19th June, 2023 at Irshalwadi village, Tahsil Khalapur, District Raigad, Maharashtra
The disastrous landslide incidence at Irshalwadi village, Khalapur Tehshil of Raigad District, Maharashtra on 19th July 2023 (around 22:30 hrs.) claimed the lives of 84 people and completely damaged nearly 32 houses. April 2024
Rock slide at Abrali village along NH-313 in Dibang Valley district, Arunachal Pradesh on 24-04-2024
On 24.04.2024 a large rock slide triggered at Abrali village along NH-313 (Lat. 28°23'21.11"N and Long. 95°55'39.94"E; Toposheet no. 82P/15) and disrupted the only communication route connecting Dibang Valley. The incessant rainfall in the area caused excess run-off along the slope, thereby causing loosening of the rock along joints resulting in a rock flow. The slide debris from the hill slope accumulated over the road causing blockage of the existing drain present along the hill side portion of the road. This surface runoff changed its course and cut across the road and washed away the main road.

Debris slide at Pernote village, Ramban district, Jammu and Kashmir on the evening of 25-04-2024
A devastating landslide struck Pernote village, Ramban district, Jammu and Kashmir on the evening of 25th April, 2024 (Lat. 33°14'32.46"N, Long. 75°12'28.76"E; Toposheet no. 43O/04). The incident caused significant damage to approximately 35 houses, sections of the Ramban-Gool road, the Pernote village link road, a 400 KV transmission line, four transmission towers (three completely destroyed and one partially damaged), a power grid station and a water supply pipeline. The slopes are covered in thick overburden material (up to 30 meters or more) composed of weathered rock fragments (sandstone and siltstone), clay and silt. The landslide is located on the MBT footwall, where thrusting has resulted in highly fractured, shattered, and folded rock masses of Lower Murree Formation and Ramban Formation.

Cyclone Remal-event triggered landslide incidences in the state of Mizoram
The Cyclone Remal brought intense rainfall from 26.05.2024 to 28.05.2024 in the state of Mizoram. Heavy rainfall in excess of 205 mm was recorded during this period in Aizawl District and nearby areas (source: State MET Centre, DST, Aizawl). This event triggered more than 300 landslides in the state of Mizoram affecting both natural and cut slopes. Landslide incidences were particularly high in the capital city of Aizawl and surrounding areas, inflicting huge loss to property and resulting in 34 casualties. Majority of the landslides are of complex type i.e., initiating as a rock or debris/earth slides and turning into flow due to high water discharge. The casualties and destruction to houses and roads are mainly due to debris flow. Inventory of 170 landslide incidences was prepared.

Earth slide at Garo Basti, 8th Mile, Kamrup (Metro) District, Assam on 05-07-2024

September 2024
Rock fall at Vaisno Devi Yatra route, Katra, District Reasi, UT: JK on 02.09.2024
A landslide occurred on September 2, 2024 along the Himkoti Marg leading to Bhawan, resulting in two fatalities and one injury, according to media reports. The slope material at site comprises highly jointed limestones (three prominent joint sets). At the time of assessment, hydrological conditions in the area were noted to be wet (water seepage along the joint planes in the rock). The recent rockfall is inferred to be caused by wedge failure triggered by extensive rainfall.
