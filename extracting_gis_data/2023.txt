April 2023
Rockslide on Shillong-Dawki road (NH-40), Ryngngain, East Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya on 13th April 2023
A rockslide occurred on 13th April 2023 along Shillong-Dawki road (NH-40), at Ryngngain, East Khasi Hills district, Meghalaya. Two people were reported dead while nine sustained injuries after a boulder fell on two vehicles. This landslide is located at Ryngngain area (N 25.365263°, E 91.878497°). The rockslide dislodged blocks of Quartzite towards road which resulted in the blockage of communication along National Highway (NH-40) connecting Shillong to Dawki road. The continuous quarrying activities at the toe of the cut slope occupied with highly jointed quartzite disturb the natural equilibrium resulted into rock slide.

June 2023
Landslide at Vaivakawn, Aizwal District, Mizoram on 09th June 2023
A landslide occurred on 09.06.2023 at Vaivakawn Veng, Aizawl, Aizawl District (23⁰ 44′ 29.43″N; 92⁰ 42′ 28.29″E) caused i) road blockage on NH 54 for three days, ii) complete burial of one pucca house under construction, iii) dismantling of one pucca house, iv) evacuation of one house and v) displaced six families. Heavy and continuous rainfall on the night of 08.06.2023 and early morning of 09.06.2023 triggered this failure. The main causal factors for this event is the seepage of water from the nala along the openings in joint planes, cut slope and excavation on both toe and crown leading to widening of joints aiding in destabilization of the slope.

Landslide at Pyndengrei Village, West Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya on 17th June 2023
A landslide occurred at Pyndengrei locality (25°31’44.45” N; 91°15’42.79” E) within Nongstoin town, West Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya in the wee hours of 17th June 2023. The debris slide claimed lives of two minor siblings, injured 3 persons and partially destroyed one wooden house. Excavation of natural slope at about 82 ̊ in highly weathered Granite and untreated slope cut are the causative factors for the landslide which was triggered due to heavy rainfall (223 mm.)

Landslide at Dhirenpara, Fatasil hill, Kamrup Metro District, Assam on 17th June 2023
A landslide occurred on 17th June 2023 in Dhirenpara area which lies in the eastern part of Fatasil Hill, Kamrup (Metro) district, Assam (26º09'01.935" N, 91º43'40.733" E). Thick section of weak overburden material, multi-tier slope cutting, haphazard flow of surface water flow during incessant rainfall, inadequate retaining wall and surcharge load from civil structures are the main causal factors that led to earth slide killing one person. The triggering factor is attributed to incessant rainfall preceding the incidence.

July & August 2023
Rock fall along NH-29 killed two people in Chumoukedima district, Nagaland on 04-07-2023
On 4th July 2023 at around 5.00 pm, a rock fall occurred near the old Check Gate along NH-29 in Chumoukedima district, Nagaland. The rock boulders fell from a height of 60-70 m and hit three vehicles plying on the NH-29. The vehicles were completely damaged. The rock fall triggered by rain killed two occupants of the vehicles and injured three others. The rock fall is located at coordinates N25.791802°& E93.803309°and falls in SoI toposheet no. 83G/13.

Landslide in Irshalwadi village, Taluk Khalapur, District Raigad, Maharashtra on 19th July 2023
A debris flow occurred in Irshalwadi village, Taluk Khalapur, District Raigad, Maharashtra on 19th July 2023 triggered by heavy, incessant rainfall (18° 55’ 58.9’’ 73° 14’ 4.1’’). The incidence damaged/buried about 18 houses of village due to slide debris and a took a toll of 26 lives. Presence of thick overburden, moderate to steep backslope/rock-overburden interface, local depressions formed the preparatory factors for the debris flow and heavy/continuous rainfall. Due to the heavy rainfall the overburden material got supersaturated resulting in decrease of shearing strength of the materials.

Landslide in Lumjingsuk Block, Madanryting, East Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya in July 2023
A minor landslide occurred in Lumjinsuk locality (Block–A) of Madanryting, Shillong, East Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya (25°33'18.3" N: 91°54'22.4" E) in July 2023. Houses have been constructed on modified slope where domestic drainage seeping into backslope led to oversaturation of overburden material. The incessant rainfall also added in the development of pore water pressure resulting in a minor slope failure. Cracks are observed in the house located beside an old slide scar making it vulnerable to slope failure. The discharge of domestic waste water should be channelised. The natural drainage course should be maintained and kept hindrance free. Construction of toe support and breast wall are advised to stabilise the houses.

Ground Cracks and Subsidence in Lindoor Village, Lahaul and Spiti District, Himachal Pradesh in August 2023
Ground cracks and land subsidence were reported from Lindoor village (32°38'56.20"N; 76°52'38.17"E), Lahaul and Spiti district of Himachal Pradesh in August 2023. The affected area forms part of an old debris/talus deposit along left bank slopes of Jahlma nala which is a tributary of Chandra Bhaga River. Ground cracks in the agricultural fields and slopes, horizontal-vertical-diagonal-step cracks in houses of village residences, ground subsidence and two active landslides ~300m to 250 m from the village area are observed in the area. This event is attributed by the hydrogeological process leading to slope failures occur during heavy rainfall in combination with or shortly after snow melt. Continuous seepage from glacier melt waters, irrigation channels, rains during the monsoon and snow accumulation in slope during winters also contribute in destabilisation of the slope.

Landslides in Mandi District, Himachal Pradesh on July, August and September 2023
In the 2023 monsoon season, Himachal Pradesh faced a notable increase in landslides, resulting in the loss of lives and damage to properties in different areas. Mandi district, in particular, saw several occurrences of landslides. The major areas affected by landslides in Mandi district are Tarna Hills, Juccain and Tathi in Sarkaghat, Riyur area in Dharampur, Narera Cloudburst in Dharampur, Phagwaon, Dhanotu and Sundernagar. A total of 24 landslides were inventoried in Mandi district.The slides were triggered by heavy, incessant rainfall in the months of July and August. Several debris slides and flows have caused extensive damages to infrastructure including houses, roads, causing blockage and cutting off communication. The debris slides are attributed to weakened slope debris due to water ingress and subsequent slope saturation by heavy rain. The absence of proper drainage network has played a crucial role in inducing the slope failure. Increased anthropogenic activities, such as ill planned construction of buildings, civil structures, and other infrastructure projects, have contributed to hill slope cutting and modification in recent years which has resulted in slope instability.

Landslides in Kullu District, Himachal Pradesh on July and August 2023
An incessant rainfall event during the month of July - August 2023 in and around the area of Kullu, Banjar, Anni and Manali Sub-Divisions of Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh resulted in a series of slope failures/stability issues. A drastic increase in rainfall in the month of July as compared to previous years is attributed to be the triggering factor for the occurrence of these slides. An inventory of 23 landslides was prepared in the Kullu district. Concentration of pore water pressure in overburden due to damp and dripping hydrology condition coupled with further water saturation from surface runoff during incessant rainfall, disturbance of natural slope by road cutting, absence of proper drainage system on the upslope and toe cutting by streams are some of the causative factors for these slides. A number of houses, public utility structures were partially or wholly damaged, several roads (particularly NH 305) were partially or completely damaged, cutting off communication to several villages.

October 2023
Earth/mud slide that buried alive a family of four at Pynthor Langtein in West Jaintia Hills district, Meghalaya on 08-10-2023
A mud slide occurred at Pynthor Langtein in West Jaintia Hills district, Meghalaya on the midnight of 08-10-2023 and buried alive a family of four. The mud slide located at coordinates 25°25’10.4” N; 92°09’43.0” E (toposheet 83C03) is attributed to unplanned dumping of excavated earth material from the house under construction at the crown part and their mobilization during incessant rains.
